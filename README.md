# Jakia Sultana

Education researcher from Bangladesh working at the intersection of generative AI, metacognition, assessment, and learning sciences.

## About

I am a Research & Grants Lead at Altlearn and a Master of Education candidate at BRAC University. My work focuses on understanding when generative AI strengthens learning and when it begins to replace independent thinking.

## Research interests

- Generative AI and learning sciences
- AI-mediated metacognition and self-regulated learning
- Cognitive offloading and cognitive autonomy
- Assessment validity in AI-assisted learning
- Human-centered AI in education
- Teacher professional development and AI literacy
- Equity and responsible AI in education
